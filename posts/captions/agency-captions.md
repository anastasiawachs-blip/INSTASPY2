## Post 6 — Business and Partner Due Diligence

Before a partner, investor, executive, vendor or counterparty is given money, ownership, access or authority, the underlying facts should be checked.

A due-diligence scope may include the corporate entity, ownership, founder or executive history, litigation, professional claims, regulatory records, business footprint and documents supplied in support of the transaction.

We document what can be verified, what conflicts with the record, and what remains unresolved.
